# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord application/bot ("the Bot") handles information:

1. **Data Processed:** When interacting with Bot commands or reactions (such as role-signup boards), the Bot may temporarily process Discord IDs (user, server, channel, and message IDs), display names/usernames, reaction emojis, and role assignments needed to perform the requested action.
2. **Storage & Retention:** Active board state is kept in memory while the Bot is running (or only for as long as needed to update tracked messages). The Bot does not intentionally sell or rent user data to third parties.
3. **Discord Platform:** Interactions on Discord are also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy.
4. **Contact / Data Requests:** For questions contact the bot developer via Discord @xoomies
